One line, 180 characters or fewer: a museum placard for an ordinary office object, written as if it were an ancient artifact from a lost civilisation — my entry for make.fun's "Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest".

> Stapler, c. 1998 CE. Black-enamelled steel, ritual binder of leaves. Its bite has outlasted every promise stapled beneath it. Donated by the Department of Unfinished Business.

The stapler is recast as a ritual object: a catalogue date, a material, and a donor whose name does the joking, all in a deadpan curator's voice.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` validates the line against the gate (exactly one line, 1–180 characters, non-blank), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it.
